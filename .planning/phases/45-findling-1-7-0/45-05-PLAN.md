- Wiederzulassen: `A storage you include again is indexed on the next regular comparison run.`
- Leer: `No external storage has been set up by an admin.`
- files_external aus: `External storage is not enabled on this server.`
- Unterscheidung: `ID %s`
- Bestätigung eins: `Remove indexed content? Excluding the external storage %s also removes its already indexed documents from the index. The files themselves stay untouched on the storage.`
- Bestätigung mehrere: `Remove indexed content? Excluding the external storages %s also removes their already indexed documents from the index. The files themselves stay untouched on the storage.`
